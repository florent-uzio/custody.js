Sync OpenAPI specs to Custody 1.43.9 (and 1.34.29) and regenerate types. 1.43.9 adds the `GET /v1/domains/{domainId}/omnibus/{omnibusId}/transfers` operation (`getOmnibusTransfers`) and the `Omnibus_JsonNullableOffsetDateTime`, `Omnibus_JsonNullableOmnibusTransferOperation`, `Omnibus_JsonNullableString`, `Omnibus_JsonNullableTenantRef`, `Omnibus_OmnibusTransferPageResponse`, `Omnibus_OmnibusTransferStructure` and `Omnibus_OmnibusTransferSummary` schemas. 1.34.29 has no API changes. The known-version capability map gains `1.43.9` and `1.34.29`.

The SDK exposes the new endpoint as `client.omnibus.listTransfers(params, query?)`.
